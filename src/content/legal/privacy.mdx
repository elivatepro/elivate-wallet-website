## What we collect

When your team uses Meridian, we collect the telemetry you send us — traces, metrics, logs, and the events your agents emit. We collect the operational context that surrounds them: workspace name, member identities, billing details, and the configuration you save in the console.

We collect basic product usage data so we can keep the console quiet — page paths, agent versions, browser type, and the shape of your interactions. We do not collect the contents of pages you visit outside Meridian.

## What we use it for

We use your data to render the console, run the SLO engine, and draft the post-mortems your engineers asked us to draft. We use it to bill the workspace, contact account owners, and notify you when an incident touches your data.

We never sell your telemetry. We never use the contents of your traces to train shared models. We do not look at your data unless you ask us to, or unless we have to in order to keep the service running.

## Where it lives

Telemetry is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each workspace's data is isolated at the storage layer. We retain telemetry for the window your plan specifies and delete it on schedule — older spans are not waiting in a backup tape somewhere.

We host on SOC 2 Type II infrastructure in the regions you select at workspace creation. Member access is logged; engineering access requires a paired review and an audit trail.

## Your rights

You can export, redact, or delete any data your workspace has sent us. Account owners can do this from the console; member-level requests route through the workspace admin. Deleted workspaces purge within 30 days, including from cold storage.

If you operate in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you have additional rights — access, portability, restriction, objection. Write to privacy@meridian.systems and we'll route it to a human within two business days.

## Cookies & analytics

The marketing site uses essential cookies for sign-in and a small set of privacy-respecting analytics cookies for traffic shape. The console uses session cookies only — no third-party advertising trackers, ever.

You can refuse non-essential cookies on your first visit and change your mind from the footer of any page.

## Subprocessors

We rely on a short list of vetted infrastructure providers — cloud compute, transactional email, payments, and error tracking. Each one is bound by a data processing agreement that mirrors the protections in this policy. The current list is published at meridian.systems/legal/subprocessors and updated at least 30 days before any addition takes effect.

## Children

Meridian is built for engineering teams. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If a workspace contains data that should not be there, write to privacy@meridian.systems and we'll remove it.

## Changes

We update this policy when we change how we handle data. Material changes get an in-product notice and an email to workspace owners 30 days before they take effect. Continued use after that date constitutes acceptance.
