Elivate Wallet gives every member a clear view of their balance and transactions, while leaders manage income, withdrawals, teams, and financial activity from one place.

## How your network is organized

Your network sits at the top. Offices and teams sit under it, and members belong to an office. Leaders see the members and activity below them; members see their own wallet.

## What members see

- Their available balance
- Income and expenses tied to their record
- Their transaction history
- The status of any withdrawal they request

## What leaders do

- Record income against the right member, with receipts attached
- Review withdrawal requests and record payouts
- Follow activity and records across the offices they manage
