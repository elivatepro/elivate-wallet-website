A withdrawal starts in Wallet as a request from the member, so it is recorded against their account from the beginning.

## The stages

1. **Requested.** The member submits the amount and payout account.
2. **Approved.** A leader approves it. The amount is reserved, but the balance has not changed yet.
3. **Payout recorded.** The leader pays from their bank and records what was sent, with the transfer receipt.
4. **Verified.** The receipt is checked and the withdrawal is completed.

## Where to see it

Members follow each request from their withdrawals list. Leaders work from the withdrawal queue, grouped by what needs action.
