Income is recorded against one member, so their balance and history update in the same place.

## Record an entry

1. Choose the member.
2. Enter the amount, currency, source, and date received.
3. Attach the receipt.
4. Review the split, then post.

## After it posts

The member sees the new income in their balance and activity without asking for an update. The entry keeps its receipt, so it can be checked later.
